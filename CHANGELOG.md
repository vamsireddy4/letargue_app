# Changelog

All notable changes to the LetArgue app. Downloads are on the [Releases](../../releases) page.

## 0.1.0 (2026-10-15)

First version, for Mac and Windows.

- One model or Debate: several AIs answer, argue until they agree, and give one answer.
- Sign in with your own Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Grok, Muse and Kimi accounts, API keys from 25+ providers, or local AI.
- Tasks board and Group chat: team tasks with debate, plan, work, checks and a final result; join the chat any time.
- Computer use in your own browser, with approval or automatic approval, and an on/off switch.
- Instant commands for apps, sites, songs, media keys, volume and folders.
- Voice: wake name, voice key, ElevenLabs voices, choice of speech-to-text provider.
- Show me where: points at things on the screen you're using.
- Images and videos through Grok.
- Account keys encrypted and synced to your account; chats stay on your computer.
