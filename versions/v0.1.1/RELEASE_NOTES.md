## What's new
- **Intel Macs:** LetArgue now runs on Macs with an Intel processor, not just Apple silicon.
- **New app icon:** the LetArgue logo, large, on a white tile. It stays white in dark mode instead of turning into a dark tile.
- **Menu bar icon:** now a clean logo that turns white or black to match your menu bar, like the system's own icons. Before, it showed as a white square.
- **Faster computer use:** each step on the screen is about 40% quicker (Opus ~3–4 s, Haiku ~1.5 s per step).
- **Computer control switch:** turn screen control on or off in Profile → Shortcuts.
- **Instant everyday commands:** open apps, sites and folders, play songs (YouTube, YouTube Music, Apple Music, Spotify), pause and skip, volume up, down and mute. Done at once, without an AI.
- **Voice does it:** spoken requests are done on your computer; only writing goes to the chat.

### Opening it the first time
- **Mac:** open the DMG and drag **LetArgue** to **Applications**. If macOS says it can't check the app, go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**.
- **Windows:** if SmartScreen appears, click **More info → Run anyway**.
