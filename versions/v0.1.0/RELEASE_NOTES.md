### Opening it the first time
- **Mac:** open the DMG and drag **LetArgue** to **Applications**. The first time, macOS may say it can't check the app. Go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down, and click **Open Anyway** next to LetArgue. After that it opens normally.
- **Windows:** if SmartScreen says "Windows protected your PC", click **More info → Run anyway**.

## What's in it
- **One model or Debate:** several AIs answer, argue until they agree, and give you one answer.
- **Your own AI accounts:** Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Grok, Muse and Kimi sign-ins, API keys from 25+ providers, or local AI like Ollama. LetArgue doesn't sell AI.
- **Tasks and Group chat:** hand off a job; the AIs plan it, split the work, check each other and report back. Join the chat any time.
- **Computer use:** the AI works in your own browser, step by step, with Stop always there and an on/off switch.
- **Instant commands:** "open YouTube", "play Believer", "volume up", "open my Downloads": done at once.
- **Voice:** your own wake name, a voice key, ElevenLabs voices and your choice of speech to text.
- **Show me where:** points at the button you're looking for.
- **Images and video** made by your Grok account.
- **Privacy:** chats stay on your computer; only your name, email and the keys you save are kept, encrypted.
