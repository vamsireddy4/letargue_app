LetArgue is a desktop app for **Mac and Windows** that puts the AI accounts you already have (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Grok, Meta Muse, Kimi, local models and more) into one chat. They answer side by side, challenge each other, and agree on one clear answer. LetArgue doesn't sell AI: you sign in with your own accounts.

This repository tracks **LetArgue app versions**: release notes and downloads. The source code is private.

## What it does

- **Debate:** several AIs answer, argue and agree on one answer. Or use one model.
- **Your accounts:** sign in with Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Grok, Muse or Kimi, add API keys, or use local AI (Ollama).
- **Tasks and group chat:** hand off a job; the AIs plan it, split the work, check each other and report back. Join the chat any time.
- **Computer use:** the AI can do things on your screen in your own browser, step by step, with Stop always available. Can be turned off.
- **Instant commands:** "open YouTube", "play Believer", "volume up", "open my Downloads": done at once.
- **Voice:** your own wake name, a voice key, ElevenLabs voices, and the speech-to-text provider of your choice.
- **Show me where:** it points at the button you're looking for.
- **Images and video:** made by your Grok account, right in the chat.

## Privacy

Your chats stay on your computer. LetArgue's servers keep only your account: name, email and the API keys you choose to save, encrypted. See the [Privacy Policy](https://letargue.com/privacy.html).
